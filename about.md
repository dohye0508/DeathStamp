# Death Stamp

Leaves a marker of your character everywhere you died, so you can see at a glance which parts of a level keep giving you trouble.

## How to use

1. Open your level and start playing
2. Turn it on from the mod's settings, or from the pause menu (**Esc → Death Stamp: ON/OFF**)
3. Every death drops a marker at that spot
4. Play a few more attempts to build up markers across the roughest sections

## Settings

- **Death Stamp 켜기** — master switch, off by default
- **표시 진하기** — how solid the death markers look
- **재시도할 때마다 표시 지우기** — off (default) keeps every marker from every attempt; on clears them each retry
