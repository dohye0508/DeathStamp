# Death Stamp

Leaves a marker everywhere you died, so you can see at a glance which parts of a level keep giving you trouble.

## How to use

1. Open your level and start playing
2. Press **Esc** and tap the **Death Stamp** button — this opens the settings popup
3. Turn it on, and pick how you want markers to look
4. Play a few attempts to build up markers across the roughest sections

## Settings (in the popup)

- **Enabled** — master switch, off by default
- **Marker Shape** — your character icon, an X, or an O
- **Marker Size** — scale X/O markers up or down (icon markers always show at their true size)
- **Marker Color** — tap the swatch to pick any color from GD's own color wheel (X/O markers only)
- **Opacity** — how solid the markers look
- **Max Markers (0 = all)** — cap how many markers stick around at once; 0 keeps all of them
- **Clear All Markers** — wipes every marker on the level right now
