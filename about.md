# Death Stamp

Leaves a marker everywhere you died, so you can see at a glance which parts of a level keep giving you trouble.

## How to use

1. Open your level and start playing
2. Press **Esc** and tap the **Death Stamp** button — this opens the settings popup
3. Turn it on, and pick how you want markers to look
4. Play a few attempts to build up markers across the roughest sections

## Settings (in the popup)

- **Death Stamp 켜기** — master switch, off by default
- **재시도마다 표시 지우기** — off (default) keeps every marker from every attempt; on clears them each retry
- **마커 모양** — your character icon, an X, or an O
- **마커 색** — red or green, for X/O markers
- **표시 진하기** — how solid the markers look
- **최근 몇 개만 (0=전체)** — cap how many markers stick around at once; 0 keeps all of them
